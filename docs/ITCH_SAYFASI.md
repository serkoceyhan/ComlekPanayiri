## Description

A small physics game made in Unity.

Fire cannonballs at a fairground stall. Shatter every clay jar and knock the heavy stones off the tables. Cannonballs are limited, and every one you save earns an extra star.

**Controls**
- Mouse: move to aim, click to fire
- Touch: hold and drag to aim, release to fire

**Features**
- 8 levels: pyramids, twin towers, a stone wall, a sliding counter and more
- Chain reactions: break the bottom jar and watch the rest come down
- Everything is generated in code, with no imported models, textures or sounds

Source code on GitHub: https://github.com/KULLANICI_ADIN/comlek-panayiri
